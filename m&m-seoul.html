💡 Good to know
MapsGoogle Maps can't give directions in Korea — use Naver Map or Kakao Map. Every map button here opens Naver.
Getting aroundSubway is fast, cheap and beats traffic. Taxis are reasonable — Kakao T is the app. The itinerary already flags where a taxi is worth it (Seongsu → Mr. Ahn's, Hyundai → Myeon Seoul).
PayingCards accepted almost everywhere. A T-money card helps for buses; many foreign contactless cards now tap in on the subway.
Closing days ✓Gebang Sikdang closes Mondays — your visit is Sunday ✓
Myeon Seoul closes Sundays + breaks 15:00–16:00 — your visit is Monday ✓
Mid-September weatherEarly autumn — warm days (~26–28°C), cooler evenings (~19°C), low humidity. Bring a light jacket for the late nights out.
