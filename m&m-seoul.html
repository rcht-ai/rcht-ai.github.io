💡 Good to know
MapsGoogle Maps can't do directions in Korea — use Naver Map or Kakao Map. All the map buttons here open Naver.
Getting aroundSubway is fast and cheap; taxis are reasonable but bad in rush hour. Kakao T is the taxi app.
PayingCards are accepted almost everywhere. A T-money card helps for buses; many foreign contactless cards now tap in on the subway.
Closures to noteGebang Sikdang closes Mondays (your visit is Sunday ✓). Myeon Seoul closes Sundays and breaks 15:00–16:00 (your visit is Monday ✓).
Weather in mid-SeptEarly autumn — pleasantly warm days (~26–28°C), cooler evenings (~19°C), low humidity. A light jacket for the late nights out.
